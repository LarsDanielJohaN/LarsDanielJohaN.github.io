> The best thing about being a statistician is you get to play in everybody else's backyard.
> John Tukey

Hi, my name is Lars! I am deeply passionate about statistics, its use in modelling real world phenomena, and the implications of inference for informed decision making. I am particularly interested in the modelling of complex datasets, like those arising in banking risk models, financial markets, market research, environmental monitoring, astronomy, and aviation. In this regard, I've developed several applied projects with an extensive use of Python, R, SQL, and Julia ranging from insight mining from data to the creation of statistical packages for large complex datasets. My belief and conviction is that anyone, no matter the background, diserves and can understand statistical models, which is why I have a compromise with both clear communication and in writting reproducible and understandable code for different audiences. 

I am always on the look for new applied problems to tackle. When I do, in conjunction with my colaborators, I seek to do a wholistic assesment of the problem at hand and what are the insights that must be obtained. In so, I follow a four step process:
1. **Understand the context and the mission:** Which mission gives the necessity of data analytics? How is this reflected in the variables that the organization measure? What are the decisions that need to be taken and how can data inform them?
2. **Assess the data situation:** What kind of data we have and what are its characteristics? Does it allow us to answer the questions we have? Is it necessary to recollect more data and, if so, can we do it? 
3. **Make modelling decisions:** Based on our data situation and our questions, which model allows us to mine the insights we need? What assumptions need to be made? What are the risks for the reliability of the analysis? 
4. **Generate insights:** Having trained the necessary models, what relationships or dynamics do they exhibit about the data? What do they tell about the state of the original mission? What actions are required?
