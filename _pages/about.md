> We statisticians, with our specific concern for uncertainty, are even more liable than other practical men to encounter philosophy, whether we like it or not.
> Leonard J. Savage

Hi, my name is Lars! I am deeply passionate about statistics, its to model real world phenomena, and the implications of inference for informed decision making. I am particularly interested in the modelling of complex datasets, like those arising in banking risk models, financial markets, market research, environmental monitoring, astronomy, and aviation. In this regard, I've developed several applied projects with an extensive use R, Python, SQL, and Julia ranging from insights mining from data to the creation of statistical packages for large complex datasets.
